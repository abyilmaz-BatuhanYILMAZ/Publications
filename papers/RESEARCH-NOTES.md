# Research integrity and source verification

This is an academic **bibliography portfolio**, not a repository of malicious APKs or a reproduction of the experiments.

## Source priority

1. The **2026** paper's publisher DOI [10.1504/IJMC.2026.154630](https://doi.org/10.1504/IJMC.2026.154630) supports the bibliographic citation, dataset sizes, models, feature sets and highest reported accuracy. [Selçuk University](https://avid.selcuk.edu.tr/makale/8512690) supplies additional bibliographic verification.
2. The **2022** paper's [official journal article](https://www.ijisae.org/index.php/IJISAE/article/view/2010) supports its authors, citation, datasets, methods, 10-fold validation and reported accuracies. A reliable article DOI was not confirmed, so no DOI is assigned.

## Evidence claims

- 2026: 98.4% **reported** best classification accuracy (ANN, 117 features).
- 2022: 92.4% (NB) and 90.9% (SVM) **reported** classification accuracies.
- These values were produced in the respective publications, not by this repository.
- Different studies' performance numbers cannot be taken as a controlled before/after improvement.
- Journal rankings, quartiles, and citation counts are intentionally omitted because they vary across providers and dates.
- These articles investigated machine learning for Android malware detection. They did **not** claim to investigate generative AI or LLM security.

## Source and reuse policy

This repository provides metadata and original explanatory summaries. It does **not** redistribute publisher PDFs, APK binaries, malware datasets, pretrained model weights, or unpublished study source code. Any future reproducibility artifacts should be separately attributed, versioned and shared only if their licensing and redistribution terms permit it.

For citations use [references.bib](../references.bib) or [publications.json](../publications.json). For full articles consult the official journal or publisher pages.
